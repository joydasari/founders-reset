The 4-Day Structure

A Sprint.
Not a Classroom.

3 Nights · 4 Full Working Days · Morning & Evening Sessions · Residential · India

01
Reset & Build Foundation Arrive · Diagnose · Align
Morning Session Reset — Think Clearly About Your Business

Strip away the noise. Identify what truly matters and what has been draining you. Return to the original reason you started. Align your work with purpose.

Luke 14:28 — Count the cost before you build.

Evening Session Build — Lay the Right Foundation

With clarity from the morning, begin rebuilding your business model from first principles. What stays, what goes, what needs to be rebuilt from scratch.

Matthew 7:24 — Build on rock, not sand.

02
Outreach, Offer & Market Communicate · Reach · Test
Morning Session Outreach — Communicate What You Do Clearly

Shape an offer people understand and want. Create a simple web presence or landing page. Reach out to warm leads the right way — with honesty, not manipulation.

Proverbs 11:1 — Honest business builds honest relationships.

Evening Session Ads & Traffic — Test Your Ideas in the Real Market

Run simple ads without overthinking. Test your offer with real people, not assumptions. Read the results and adjust quickly. Know what works before you spend more.

Proverbs 21:5 — Steady planning leads to profit; haste leads to poverty.

03
Systems, Fix & Scale Review · Build · Grow
Morning Session Review & Fix — Remove What's Not Working

Review what has been built so far — what's working, what isn't, what is unnecessary complexity. Cut without sentiment. Remove every bottleneck you've been tolerating.

Ecclesiastes 3:6 — There is a time to keep and a time to throw away.

Evening Session Systems & Ops — Bring Order to Daily Operations

Set up simple systems that reduce manual work and daily chaos. Build processes that run without you. Bring order to what has been informal — so the business can breathe.

1 Corinthians 14:40 — Let all things be done decently and in order.

04
Scale & Your Next 90 Days Grow · Commit · Launch
Morning Session Scaling — Grow Without Creating Chaos

Understand how to grow sustainably. Use partnerships wisely. Build momentum that doesn't rely on you being present everywhere. Scale as a steward, not as someone chasing numbers.

Luke 16:10 — Faithful in small things, trusted with larger ones.

Evening Session Your Next 90 Days — Leave With a Written Plan

Review your full 4-day progress. Set clear commitments. Build a written, realistic 90-day action plan before you leave the room — one that doesn't require willpower alone to sustain.

Habakkuk 2:2 — Write the vision, make it plain.

On faith and work in this cohort: Biblical principles are not the morning devotional and then forgotten. Every session is anchored to a specific scriptural framework. This is what stewardship-based business actually looks like — not prosperity theology, not inspiration without execution. The two are inseparable here.
